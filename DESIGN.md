# Visual Identity

## Color Strategy: Committed
A deep, midnight-inspired palette with a singular, high-contrast metallic accent.

- **Primary (Midnight):** `oklch(0.15 0.02 260)` — Deep, tinted charcoal.
- **Accent (Luxe Gold):** `oklch(0.82 0.12 85)` — Refined, soft metallic gold.
- **Surface (Paper):** `oklch(0.98 0.01 85)` — Off-white, warm ivory.
- **Text (Ink):** `oklch(0.25 0.02 260)` — Soft black.

## Typography
- **Headings:** `Playfair Display`, Serif. High contrast, elegant.
- **Body:** `Inter` or `Geist Sans`, Sans-serif. Minimalist, high legibility.
- **Scale:** 1.333 ratio. Clear distinction between editorial headers and utilitarian body text.

## Layout & Motion
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace. No cramped containers.
- **Rhythm:** Varied vertical padding to create an editorial flow.
- **Motion:** `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (Quintic Ease Out). Slow, intentional entrances.

## Anti-Patterns (The Slop List)
- NO generic card grids with identical shadows.
- NO "Hero-Metric" templates (Big number $\rightarrow$ small label).
- NO gradient text or "glassmorphism" as a default.
- NO side-stripe borders on cards.
